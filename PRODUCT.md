# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Companies seeking software and applied AI to address a business opportunity or process. They need a senior engineering partner who can understand the business need and turn it into a delivered digital product or a clear technical next step.

## Product Purpose

The public portfolio and service site for Glauber Barcelos helps prospective business clients understand his capabilities, review selected work, and start a conversation about a project. Success means relevant project inquiries; no inquiry or conversion target is defined.

## Positioning

Glauber is a senior engineer who turns business processes into delivered software. His offer spans end-to-end engineering for web products, SaaS, mobile and desktop applications, and applied AI, with architecture and technical leadership available when a project needs them.

## Operating Context

The site serves companies evaluating a software project or senior technical support. Glauber is based in Porto Alegre, Brazil, and serves clients remotely across Brazil. Prospective clients review service pages and documented work, then contact him to discuss needs and scope. Commercial terms, timelines, and deliverables depend on the agreed proposal.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; there is no build step or bundler. GitHub Pages serves the site at `glauberbarcelos.com.br`.
- The site includes a portfolio homepage, service pages for web, SaaS, mobile/desktop, and senior technical advisory, plus a Native IP case page.
- The homepage supports Portuguese and English. Keep user-facing copy in both locales through the existing i18n system.
- Contact paths include WhatsApp and the site's contact form. A project diagnostic helps visitors identify a relevant next step.
- Do not promise fixed pricing, scope, or delivery timelines; these are agreed for each proposal.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Glauber Barcelos and the professional description Senior Software Engineer / Tech Lead. The portfolio serves a Brazilian audience, with Portuguese as the primary language and English also available on the homepage.

## Evidence on Hand

The homepage and service pages present current capabilities and selected work. `case-native-ip.html` documents the scope of a Native IP landing page; it does not establish that Glauber built the Native IP SaaS platform. Use only real, authorized project details and substantiated outcomes. Do not invent clients, testimonials, metrics, or claims.

## Product Principles

- Understand the business opportunity or process before choosing a technical solution.
- Deliver software that addresses the need from technical planning through implementation.
- Match the engagement to the work required, whether product development or senior technical guidance.
- Keep project descriptions and business claims accurate, evidenced, and authorized.
- Give prospective clients a clear next step to discuss fit and scope.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve the site's established accessibility behavior, including semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus, labeled controls, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. No formal conformance standard has been specified.
